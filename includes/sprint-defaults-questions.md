# Sprint defaults questions

Asked by gantry:brainstorming when it runs inside a sprint, in Refine, after the
usual questions, ONE at a time. Record the answers under "## Sprint defaults" in
the spec. They are tie-breakers, not requirements.

1. When the spec is silent on how strict a rule should be, lean toward:
   (a) strict, refuse, (b) lenient, warn, (c) ask me.
2. When a new data field is needed, lean toward:
   (a) nullable, (b) required with a default, (c) ask me.
3. When an existing behavior must change to fit the new one, lean toward:
   (a) change it, (b) add alongside it, (c) ask me.
4. Is there any area where every decision must come to you?
